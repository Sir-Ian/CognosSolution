About this policy

Cognos Solution LLC (“we,” “us”) develops software and other projects. This policy describes our general privacy practices for our company website and communications. Product-specific notices explain any additional data handling and take precedence for that product where they differ.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we receive your contact details and whatever information you include. Our website hosting providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, requested pages, and access times to deliver and secure the site.

This company website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, cookies, or browser storage. Individual products may have different practices, described in their own notices.

How we use & share information

We use information to respond to you, support the relevant service, maintain business records, protect security, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may share information with service providers supporting those purposes, when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations. Information collected for one product is not automatically available for unrelated uses.

Retention, security & your choices

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for its purpose or applicable legal requirements and use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No system is completely secure. Product-specific notices describe additional retention and deletion practices.

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete personal information, email hello@cognossolution.com and identify the relevant product. We may verify your identity or direct you to the organization responsible for your data. Some records may need to be retained by law.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to data portability, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Your rights under applicable law remain unchanged.

LayerLog

LayerLog is a hackathon app that accesses or stores Jira data. Its specific data categories, service providers, storage, and retention practices have not yet been confirmed. This general policy is not a complete LayerLog privacy notice; those details need to be disclosed before users authorize access.

You can contact us about LayerLog data or deletion requests. Revoking an app’s authorization does not by itself delete data already stored.

TapTrust

TapTrust is a commercial identity-verification product for customers. Its product-specific privacy notice needs to explain how ID barcodes, digital credentials, and related personal information are processed, transmitted, and retained. Those practices are not established by this company policy.

This company website does not collect ID scans or digital credentials. Please do not send identity documents to our general contact email.

Other sites & policy updates

External websites have their own privacy policies. Our company website is not intended for children under 13; contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through it.

We may update this policy as our practices change and will revise the date above. Material changes to product data handling will be addressed in the relevant product notice.
